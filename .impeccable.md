## Design Context

### Users
A college student (and grader) testing a cost management app. Used at a desk during development and grading. No real end-users — this is a course submission. The interface needs to be clear and functional, not impressive.

### Brand Personality
Honest, plain, unceremonious. Three words: **clear, compact, unobtrusive**.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean light-mode utility UI. No decorative elements. Comfortable reading, obvious controls, no visual noise. Closest reference: a well-formatted spreadsheet or a plain admin tool. Anti-reference: anything with gradients, cards, shadows, or "designed" elements. Fonts should be readable at small sizes without feeling clinical.

### Design Principles
1. Every pixel either carries information or creates breathing room — nothing else.
2. Hierarchy through weight and size, not color or decoration.
3. Forms feel fast to fill in — label above field, generous tap target, clear state.
4. No surprises — the interface behaves exactly as expected.
5. Neutral enough that it never distracts from the data being tested.
